Medium: a pull request review in the terminal, now with its test run: each proposal as a memory diff with quoted evidence, and beside it what an eval measured (every run a cell, the change as a confidence interval) and what the contradiction check found (both sentences, side by side).
Type: the terminal's own monospace and nothing else; in the recordings, a monospace stack (SF Mono, Menlo, DejaVu Sans Mono). No display face, no logo. Numbers carry their interval or they are not shown.
Palette: ANSI meaning only. Green added, verified, passed, helps (▲); red removed, not found, failed, worse (▼), contradicts (≠); yellow needs a second look; cyan keys and commands; dim for metadata and anything inferred. With NO_COLOR every mark and word stays; only the highlighting goes.
Motion: the screen changes only when you press a key or a run finishes, and each change is evidence: a cell fills, the interval on the −100 to +100 axis narrows. Clocks tick once a second. Recordings play one frame at a time and hold still under prefers-reduced-motion.
Refused: banners and ASCII art, emoji, spinners, progress theatre, a percentage without its interval, a verdict without its runs, boxes around everything, fake window chrome, and a TUI framework.
